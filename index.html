01 / THE BRANDBorn in China · Thinking globally

You’re building more
than possibilities.

embuddies is a global co-creation platform for young embodied AI creators.

We’re looking for people who go deep into the technology—and get close to the people who will use it. From a first prototype to a useful product, your understanding of the problem, your decisions and the work you make happen deserve to be seen.

embuddies brings together embodied AI and buddies. Through hackathons, shared projects and new connections, we help promising young creators find each other and bring ideas into the real world.

Meet you

Start with your work. Tell us about the problems you’re solving, what you’ve tried and what you still want to build.

Try it together

Find a real setting for your work. Explore what it can do alongside users, companies and technical partners.

Keep creating

Join a team, take a project further or start something of your own. Build your next step around what matters to you.
