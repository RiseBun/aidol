EMBUDDY HACKATHON · SEASON 01 · SHANGHAI

First season in the making

Let your creativity
be seen.

An embodied AI talent showcase where the work takes center stage.

Bring your creation to the stage. Give us a name to remember.
First stop: Shanghai.

This is a standalone embuddies event, bringing creators together through hands-on building, creator stories, campus support and live showcases. It is open to university students and young developers across China, with selected participants joining us in Shanghai. Come with an idea or an existing prototype. Show what you can build, and let every team member’s contribution be seen.

01 / THE CHALLENGELet the work speak.

Bring your idea.
Build your standout work.

Where you started and what you pushed forward both deserve to be part of the story.

Pick your challenge

Teams will choose from a set of embodied AI challenges. Themes will be confirmed alongside equipment and partner resources.

Two tracks. Room to shine.

Physical demos and simulation projects are both welcome. Separate judging tracks will account for their different conditions.

Get ready to build

We plan to provide some equipment, and teams may bring their own. Loan options, requests and requirements for personal equipment will be announced before the event.

Disclose any work completed before the event and document what you add during it. Team jury awards will focus on the final product.

02 / THE SEASON JOURNEYFrom the lineup to the spotlight

From your first showcase
to your debut moment.

The lineup and team formation begin before the event.
First showcases, building, demos and the final happen on site.

  1. 01

    The open call

    Ready for your moment?

    Bring an idea, or bring your team. Individuals and existing teams can apply once applications open. Selected solo creators will be matched with teammates before the event to compete as teams with a shared project.

  2. 02

    The lineup reveal

    Remember these names.

    Meet the creators and teams, their backgrounds, roles and project ideas. Audience voting is planned to open alongside the lineup announcement.

  3. 03

    The first showcase

    First appearance. Make it yours.

    Introduce your project, show what you already have and share the breakthrough you’re aiming for. Once the building begins, document each iteration and everyone’s contribution.

  4. 04

    The final stage

    Bring your best. Go for your debut.

    Every team presents its work. The jury selects the main finalist lineup, with one extra audience wildcard decided by votes. Finalists make their case through live demos, project pitches and Q&A.

  5. 05

    The season honors

    The work. The names. Their moment.

    We’ll reveal the team and individual jury awards, with audience honors presented separately. Team jury awards focus on the final product; individual award categories and criteria will be announced later.

03 / CHEER THEM ONBack your favorite creators

Your support.
Their shot at the final.

One audience wildcard sits alongside the jury-selected finalists.
Your votes can send one more team to the final stage.

One vote. A whole team.

Audience votes are cast for teams, not individuals. Team popularity awards and individual jury awards are judged separately.

The wildcard vote closes after team demos

Voting for the wildcard closes after all teams have presented. Once jury-selected finalists are announced, the remaining eligible team with the most votes takes the extra place.

The jury’s choice. The crowd’s favorite.

The jury decides the professional awards. The voting period and deadline for popularity awards are still to be confirmed. Voting limits, verification and tie-break rules will be published before voting opens.

Voting opens later

This is an outline of the planned format. The voting link and full rules will be published before the event. Event honors and creator assessments are recorded separately; votes do not determine job or project-support opportunities.

04 / AFTER THE DEBUTBeyond the spotlight

The season ends.
Your story keeps going.

There will be team and individual awards.
Individual award categories and criteria are still to come.

Let people meet the makers

With participants’ permission, creator stories, project features and build updates will capture the season’s experiments and breakthroughs—and the people behind them.

Keep the work moving

After the stage, keep building what you believe in. We hope to help you meet new collaborators and find your next chance to try something.
